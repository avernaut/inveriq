# Backup Demo Video Plan

## Objective

Create a 90–120 second local video that reproduces the exact live demo narrative.

The video must not depend on Internet access.

## Recording setup

Record:

- dashboard only;
- 1920×1080 if possible;
- no notifications;
- no terminal unless deliberately shown;
- no mouse wandering;
- no personal information.

## Shot list

### 0–10 s

INVERIQ idle dashboard.

Text visible:

**Verified Autonomous Defense**  
**AI decides. INVERIQ verifies.**

### 10–25 s

Brute-force attack appears.

Show:

- Credential Brute Force;
- MITRE T1110;
- confidence;
- target.

### 25–45 s

Candidate mitigation comparison appears.

Pause long enough to read.

### 45–65 s

Unsafe candidate becomes REJECTED.

Emphasize:

- reachability fail;
- availability fail;
- blast radius fail.

### 65–85 s

Safe candidate becomes VERIFIED.

Show V1–V6 all passing.

### 85–105 s

Enforcement and post-verification.

Show:

- attack reduction;
- availability;
- blast radius;
- response latency.

### Final frame

**INVERIQ by Avernaut**  
**AI decides. INVERIQ verifies.**  
`avernaut.com`
